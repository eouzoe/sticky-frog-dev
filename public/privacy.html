Sticky Frog – Privacy

Sticky Frog collects anonymous play statistics so we can see which levels are too hard, too easy, or make people leave, and fix them. We do not collect personal data and there is no account or login.

What we collect

  • A random anonymous ID stored in your browser (localStorage), and a random ID for each visit. They are not linked to your name, email, or any account.
  • Game events: session start, level start, death (level, position, progress, cause, seconds), level clear (seconds, stars, deaths, flies), leaving a level (where, and whether you went back to the menu or closed or switched away from the tab), and Endless-mode results (distance, score).
  • Technical basics: game version, interface language, desktop or mobile, window size, graphics quality, load time, and your country (derived by our server host from the connection).

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or any personal information. No advertising ID. No cookies.
  • Your IP address is not stored. Your browser's user-agent string is not stored (only desktop vs. mobile).
  • No data from other sites or apps.

Why

Only to improve level design and keep the game fun. The data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1).

How to turn it off

Add ?notelemetry to the game's address (for example https://…/?notelemetry) and nothing is sent. You can also clear this site's data in your browser to discard your anonymous ID.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: please contact the developer through the game's CrazyGames page.

隱私權說明（繁體中文）

Sticky Frog 會蒐集匿名的遊玩統計，用來知道哪些關卡太難、太簡單或讓人離開，進而改進。我們不蒐集個人資料，也沒有帳號或登入。

蒐集：瀏覽器內的隨機匿名 ID（localStorage）與每次造訪的隨機 ID；遊戲事件（開始遊戲、開始關卡、死亡的關卡／位置／進度／死因／秒數、過關的秒數／星數／死亡次數、離開關卡的位置與方式、無盡模式的距離與分數）；以及遊戲版本、介面語言、桌機或手機、視窗大小、畫質、載入時間，和由伺服器主機依連線判斷的國家。

不蒐集：姓名、電子郵件等任何個資、廣告 ID、cookie。不儲存 IP 位址，也不儲存瀏覽器的 user agent（只記桌機或手機）。

用途：僅用於改進關卡設計。資料不會販售或提供給第三方，存放於 Cloudflare（Workers 與 D1）。

如何關閉：在遊戲網址後加上 ?notelemetry，就不會送出任何資料；清除瀏覽器的網站資料即可丟棄匿名 ID。
